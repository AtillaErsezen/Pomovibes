# Pomovibes Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Pomovibes is a Pomodoro timer browser extension. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Everything stays in your own browser, using Chrome's extension storage:

- **On this device** (`chrome.storage.local`): the timer state, your to-do list and categories, completed tasks, your study calendar history, and a custom bell sound if you upload one.
- **Your preferences** (`chrome.storage.sync`): timer durations, dark mode, the floating timer and background sound toggles, and the sound type and volume. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome copies these preferences to your other signed-in Chrome browsers. That copying is done by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer never receives this data.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to a server. The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party services.
- It does not read the content of the websites you visit. The floating timer script only adds a small countdown to the page, and you can turn it off.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data from your browser.
